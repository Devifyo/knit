# 1. Getting Started

This is the first thing to read. By the end you'll have an account, you'll be logged in,
and you'll know how to move around Knit.

---

## What Knit is

Knit is a CRM — a single place for your whole team to manage customers, sales, support,
marketing, and projects. Everything your company creates lives in your own private
**workspace**; no other company can see it.

---

## Create your workspace (sign up)

This is for the **first person** at your company. (If a teammate invited you instead,
skip to [Joining a workspace by invitation](#joining-a-workspace-by-invitation).)

1. Go to the sign-up page (**/register**), titled *"Create your workspace."*
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field | What to enter | Required |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Workspace name** | Your company name, e.g. *Acme Inc.* | Yes |
   | **Your name** | Your full name | Yes |
   | **Work email** | The email you'll log in with | Yes |
   | **Password** | At least 8 characters | Yes |
   | **Confirm** | The same password again | Yes |

3. Click **Create workspace**.

That's it. Behind the scenes Knit instantly sets up everything you need: your workspace,
the four standard roles (Owner, Admin, Manager, Agent), a ready-to-use **sales pipeline**
(New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won → Lost), and a sample custom field. You
become the **Owner** (full control) and land straight on your Dashboard.

---

## Joining a workspace by invitation

If a teammate sent you an invite link:

1. Open the link they shared (it looks like **/invite/…**). No account needed yet.
2. The page greets you with the workspace name and the role you're being given.
3. Fill in **Your name**, **Password**, and **Confirm password** (min 8 characters).
4. Click **Join workspace**.

You're logged in immediately. Your role (Owner/Admin/Manager/Agent) was chosen by whoever
invited you and decides what you can do — see [Team & Roles](14-team-and-roles.md).

> **Invite links expire after 7 days.** If yours doesn't work, ask the person who invited
> you to send a fresh one.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to the login page (**/login**), titled *"Welcome back."*
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. (Optional) Tick **Remember me** to stay signed in.
4. Click **Sign in**.

Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and click **Email
reset link**. Open the email, choose a new password, and you're back in.

> For your security, login is limited to 5 attempts per minute. If you get locked out for
> a moment, just wait and try again.

---

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA adds a one-time code from a phone app on top of your password. You may **choose** to
turn it on, or your workspace Owner/Admin may **require** it.

- **If your workspace requires 2FA** and you haven't set it up, Knit will send you to the
  security page to enroll before you can do anything else. This is normal — just follow
  the prompts.
- **Setting it up and the login code screen** are covered step-by-step in
  [Security & Compliance](16-security-and-compliance.md).

---

## A quick tour of the screen

Once you're in, the layout is always the same:

- **Left sidebar** — your main navigation, grouped into *Sales, Communicate, Support,
  Marketing, Collaborate, Automate* (and *Industry* if you've enabled any modules). The
  group you're currently in is highlighted.
- **Settings** (lower in the sidebar) — workspace admin: Branding, Members, Billing,
  Security, Audit log, Developer, Modules.
- **Top bar** — your workspace switcher, a **dark-mode toggle** (your choice is
  remembered on your device), and a quick-jump command palette.
- **Main area** — the page you're on.

> **Don't see a menu item?** Items appear based on your **role**. For example, an Agent
> won't see Billing or the Audit log. That's expected — see [Team & Roles](14-team-and-roles.md).
