# 6. Accounts

**What it's for:** Accounts are your key customer organisations, tracked with a **health
score** and a **renewal status**. Think of them as the high-value companies you want to
keep an eye on for retention and renewals.

**Where to find it:** Left sidebar → **Sales** → **Accounts**.

---

## Reading the accounts list

Accounts is a **read-only overview**. The table shows, for each account:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Account** | The company name |
| **Industry** | Its industry |
| **Health** | A 0–100 score — green (70+), orange (40–69), red (below 40) |
| **Renewal** | The upcoming renewal date |
| **Status** | *renewed* (green), *at risk* / *pending* (orange), *churned* (red), or blank |

If you don't have any yet, you'll see: *"No accounts yet — Accounts wrap your key
companies with health and renewal tracking."*

---

## Tips & gotchas

- **This screen has no create/edit buttons** — accounts and their health/renewal data are
  maintained behind the scenes from your companies and activity, not typed in here.
- **Use it as a watchlist.** Scan for red health or *at risk* renewals and act through the
  related [contacts](02-contacts-and-companies.md) and [deals](04-deals-and-pipeline.md).
